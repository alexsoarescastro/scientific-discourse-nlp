# Provenance and scope

This repository is a cleaned, submission-aligned documentation package for the semantic and scientometric workflow described in the associated manuscript.

## What is represented here

The public workflow documents:
- text preprocessing;
- SciBERT contextual document representation;
- BERTopic topic modeling;
- five-dimensional UMAP reduction within the topic-modeling pipeline;
- HDBSCAN density-based clustering;
- c-TF-IDF topic representation through BERTopic;
- a separate two-dimensional UMAP projection for semantic visualization;
- descriptive scientometric and temporal summaries.

## What is not claimed

This repository does not claim to:
- measure or predict technological maturity;
- classify technology readiness levels;
- use Random Forest or SHAP in the submitted analytical framework;
- use lexical density, MATTR, hedging, or boosting as submitted indicators;
- automatically infer the four higher-order thematic domains;
- redistribute copyrighted publication full text;
- reproduce every manuscript result without the authorized study corpus and reviewed mappings.

The four higher-order thematic domains are an interpretive aggregation of the recovered topic structure. Domain-level longitudinal analyses require the reviewed topic-to-domain mapping used by the authors.

Where the exact historical retrieval query, source-document access conditions, or other provenance details are not encoded in the public package, they are not reconstructed or invented here.
